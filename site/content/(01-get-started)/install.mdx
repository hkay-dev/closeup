---
title: Install
description: Download Closeup, move it to Applications, and get past the macOS warning the first time you open it.
---

There's no signed download yet. Download `Closeup.dmg` from the [releases page](https://github.com/hkay-dev/closeup/releases), or [build it yourself](/build).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the app">
    Open the disk image, drag **Closeup** onto the **Applications** folder next to it, then eject the disk image. Open the app from Applications, not from the disk image or Downloads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try to open it once">
    Double-click the app. macOS says "Closeup" Not Opened. Click **Done**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow it in System Settings">
    Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security** (scroll down the sidebar to find it), then scroll to the bottom of the page. Next to "Closeup" was blocked to protect your Mac, click **Open Anyway**. The button shows for about an hour after the last step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the Open "Closeup"? dialog, click **Open Anyway**. The password box takes a few seconds to appear. Enter your password. The app opens and Setup appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

:::note[Why macOS blocks it]
The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized, because the project doesn't have a paid Apple Developer ID yet. Each new version is a new unsigned build, so you do this again after an update.
:::

Next, [Setup](/setup) walks you through the rest.
