---
title: Wi-Fi
description: Pair the phone once with a QR code and it connects by itself whenever it's on the same Wi-Fi.
---

A cable keeps the phone charged and gives the steadiest picture, but you can also go without one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pairing window">
    In the menu, choose **Phone › Connect a Phone over Wi-Fi…**. A window with a QR code opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pair the phone">
    On the phone, open **Developer options › Wireless debugging** and tap **Pair device with QR code**, then point it at the window. You can also type the phone's six-digit pairing code into the window instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the connection">
    The window says when the phone is connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You only pair once. After that the phone connects by itself whenever it's on the same Wi-Fi, and the Phone menu shows a Wi-Fi or cable symbol next to each phone.

On the author's home network, 4K at 60 still ran at 59 fps over Wi-Fi.

## USB or Wi-Fi

**Phone › Connection** picks the link. **Wi-Fi** uses Wi-Fi whenever the phone has Wireless debugging on, even with the cable in. **USB** only uses phones on a cable, so a plugged-in phone goes over USB with Wireless debugging still on. If the webcam is live, it moves to the new link by itself and the call keeps its picture. **Settings › Phone › Phones over Wi-Fi** is the same switch.
