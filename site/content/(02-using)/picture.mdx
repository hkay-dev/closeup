## Capture mode

**Capture Mode** in the menu picks the resolution and frame rate. The default keeps the phone coolest.

| Mode | Video from the phone | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1080p at 30 fps | HEVC at 12 Mbps | The default, and the coolest for the phone |
| 1080p at 60 fps | HEVC at 20 Mbps | |
| 4K at 60 fps | HEVC at 40 Mbps | Still ran at 59 fps over Wi-Fi on a home network |
| 1080p at 120 fps | High-speed capture at 40 Mbps | <Badge variant="warning">Experimental</Badge> |

Phones differ. If yours can't run a mode, the app says so instead of quietly picking another one. Pick a lower mode and start again.

:::note
OBS's camera always tells call apps that it's 1080p at 60, even when it gets 4K frames. That's how the stock OBS extension works, and Closeup inherits it.
:::

## Orientation

**Orientation** sets how you hold the phone.

- **Landscape** (the default) is sideways.
- **Portrait** is upright. Call apps always get a wide picture, so the tall picture sits between black bars.
- **Auto-Rotate** follows the phone through all four turns, for walking around with it in your hand.

Either way round works. The phone's motion sensor tells the app which way is up, so the picture never comes out upside down. A change shows in the live picture at once, from the menu or from [Live Controls](/live-controls).

If Landscape is picked and you hold the phone upright, the menu and Live Controls point it out.
