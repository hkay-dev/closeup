---
title: Uninstall
description: Remove Closeup, its files, adb and OBS's camera extension, and turn off USB debugging on the phone.
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Quit the app">
    Quit Closeup from its menu. If you turned on **Launch at login**, turn it off in Settings first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop the adb server">
    adb keeps running after the app quits. In Terminal, run:

    ```sh
    "$HOME/Library/Application Support/Closeup/platform-tools/adb" kill-server
    ```

    If it says "cannot connect to daemon", adb wasn't running, which is fine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete the app and its files">
    ```sh
    rm -rf /Applications/Closeup.app \
      "$HOME/Library/Application Support/Closeup" \
      "$HOME/Library/Application Support/com.harsha.closeup" \
      "$HOME/Library/Caches/com.harsha.closeup" \
      "$HOME/Library/HTTPStorages/com.harsha.closeup"
    defaults delete com.harsha.closeup
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove OBS, if you don't use it">
    Drag OBS to the Trash. Finder says OBS is hosting a system extension. Click **Continue** and enter your password. The camera extension is fully gone after the next restart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn off USB debugging">
    On the phone, open **Settings › Developer options**, tap **Revoke USB debugging authorizations**, and turn off USB debugging.
  </Step>
</Steps>
